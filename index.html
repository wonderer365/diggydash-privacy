Diggy Dash – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Diggy Dash is developed by Medverk AB (Sweden). We respect your privacy.

Data we collect

None. Diggy Dash does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it works fully offline.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (completed rounds and stars) is saved only locally on your device. It never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Integritetspolicy (svenska)

Diggy Dash samlar inte in några personuppgifter. Inga konton, ingen reklam, ingen analys och inga köp i appen. Spelets framsteg sparas endast lokalt på din enhet. Kontakt: info@medverk.se
